\begin{frame}{Limitations and what comes next}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{Honest limits}
        \begin{itemize}\small
          \item \textbf{Small cohort}: 6 datasets --- proof of concept.
          \item \textbf{Localization rule is a hypothesis} --- needs validation against arthroscopy.
          \item Automatic seed may fail on atypical positioning.
        \end{itemize}
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{Why the design still helps}
        Outputs are \alert{geometric, visualized, and auditable}: the system fails \emph{loudly}, not silently --- the property a radiologist needs to trust it.
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}
