\begin{frame}{Why fat-suppressed axial MRI?}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{Two practical choices}
        \begin{itemize}
          \item \alert{Fat suppression}: fat appears dark, so tendons, edema and joint fluid stand out.
          \item \alert{Axial plane}: the humeral head appears as \emph{nested circles}, peaking at the equator.
        \end{itemize}
      \end{block}
      \begin{block}{What this gives us}
        A clean, bell-shaped \emph{area-vs-slice} curve --- the anchor of our entire pipeline.
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
