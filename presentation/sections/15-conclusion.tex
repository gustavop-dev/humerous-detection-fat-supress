\begin{frame}{Take-home}
  \begin{block}{Three ideas to remember}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item A \alert{frozen foundation model} (SAM) plus \alert{classical geometry} produces interpretable shoulder MRI features --- with no training.
      \item Two features --- a \alert{global sphere} + \alert{per-slice articular circles} --- turn the contour into a \alert{1D tendon map}.
      \item Robust across SIEMENS, GE and Philips $\Rightarrow$ useful in low-resource radiology settings.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}
